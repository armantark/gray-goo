Rules for the day

  • I commit to master after each ticket, and I do not push.
  • I look at game screenshots of every layout and every feel change before I call it done.
  • For the 3D model, I look at renders from four angles after each revision. GPT-6 Astra scores it, and GLM 5.3 Flash gives a second vote.
  • Hostile movers, a fifth level, and a level editor are out of scope.
  • Your saved speed of 200% becomes four times the old speed after ticket 05. Set the slider to 100% once.
