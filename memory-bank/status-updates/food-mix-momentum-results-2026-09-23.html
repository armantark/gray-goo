This page covers your feedback after your first play of Sugar Water. Opus 5.5 agents rebuilt all four levels to the new rules. The rules are in memory-bank/systemPatterns.md under "Food mix and momentum". The macOS app is exported again from master.

Your feedback and the change

"scattered edible objects to be like 80% of the required food … hand placed"
Placed food now gives 73% to 85% of each view's growth in every level. The world check fails a level outside 70% to 90%.
"waiting around … until enough things to spawn is really boring"
The play-through script now measures the longest wait between meals. Most waits are under 5 s.
"the spawns were supposed to be offscreen"
Spawn points release food only outside the current camera view.
"Pad the time by making it feed you less size"
Each level has a growth scale from 0.145 to 0.35. A meal gives that share of its volume.
"protons and neutrons and electrons should be simple spheres"
After late size jumps, nucleons, snails, coral branches, skateboards, and spirals show as one simple shape.
"the procedural body sucks … retire it"
It is removed, with its menu option. The shell is the only body.
"just run a simulated sped up run"
Headless Godot slept 6.9 ms each frame. The script now turns that off, and scripts/route.sh runs all levels at once.
"Why is he being stretched so long"
My speed fix left the rear grips on the ground. The grips now move with the body.
