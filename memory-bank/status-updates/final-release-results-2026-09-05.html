GAMEPLAY + ART

The finished release

  • The hermit crab, skater, and skateboard have refined models. Boards now fit the riders.
  • The initial miniature molecule is gone. Tiny targets retire as the goo grows.
  • Equal-size particles use equal eating thresholds. Depleted sugar structures disappear with their last visible part.
  • Shell and Procedural remain available. The procedural body's grip and roll remain experimental.
