What changed for the player

Eating and collision

  • The goo eats any object that looks smaller than itself, on first contact. Objects that look larger block it.
  • The goo slides along large objects and does not stick or climb over them.
  • When you eat a composite whole, its remaining parts go with it. Parts do not fall through the ground.
  • Level 1 food gave far too much growth. Emptied molecules paid growth twice. This is fixed.

Feel

  • The default speed is two times the old default.
  • Each meal pulls in with a sound, a pulse, and a burst. Big meals are bigger.
  • An object flashes when it becomes edible. The arrow grows when its target gives more food.
  • A size jump has a short slow moment, a smooth zoom out, and the tier name. The camera looks ahead.
  • The "last eaten" card now shows composites such as the skateboard.

Levels

  • Sugar Water: a gamma ray splits at the start. Particle tracks carry atoms, and the sucrose is the last big meal.
  • Tide Pool: a rock rim, a coral colony around the crown, and animals in groups along the south side.
  • Skatepark Bowl: the bowl with litter in its gutter, benches with gear piles, a street plaza, and vehicles that drive in and park.
  • Cosmic Web: four filaments meet at the great node. Galaxies stream along them. The spacetime fabric is removed.

Music and builds

  • Four new bossa nova, samba, and jazz tracks, played by Muse Sounds with brass and a sampled upright bass.
  • Skaters no longer turn brown when you eat part of them.
  • The macOS app, the download zip, and the browser build are exported again. A browser play of each level showed no errors and no cut-off text.
