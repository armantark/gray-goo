PRE-IMPLEMENTATION PLAN · 2026-09-05

Every level is a place

The four demo levels read as random scatter. This plan gives each level one place, a five-rung ladder with four size jumps, textured models, and a 3D background. Astra rebuilds the levels from the contract. Fable fixes the speed law and rebuilds the HUD.

01 The root rule

A level is one recognizable place, and every object in it belongs to a whole that the player can see. Nothing is loose unless something in the scene made it loose.

Nested wholes

Every tier of food is a visible part of the next tier. Eat parts out of a whole, then eat the whole. The whole then becomes ordinary food.

The whole was always there

The largest structure is visible from the first frame as background or as an object the goo cannot yet eat. Jumps reveal that it is food.

Damage shows

When the goo eats a part, the whole visibly changes. A nucleus that loses a proton becomes a different element. A coral that loses polyps turns white.

03 Fable's half: speed and HUD

Speed law

Today the goo crosses twelve body lengths per second at the start and under three at the goal. New law: a constant body-length rate with a smoothing curve matched to the animation. The slider stays as the fine control.

Specimen readout HUD

The level is a sample on a slide. A round gauge shows the current and goal diameter on one scale and re-scales when the unit prefix changes. A ladder strip replaces the goal bar and lights the tiers the goo can eat now. The last meal is a specimen label.

04 Order of work

  1. Generalize size jumps to a list and add the tier list to each scene config.ASTRA
  2. Bake procedural textures in Blender and add an albedo uniform to the toon shader. Regenerate all models.ASTRA
  3. Rebuild Sugar Water, Tide Pool, Skatepark Bowl, and Cosmic Web, one level per commit, with new models.ASTRA
  4. Add the four 3D backgrounds and the realistic space look.ASTRA
  5. Apply the constant body-length speed law with smoothing. Runs in parallel with step 1.FABLE
  6. Rebuild the HUD as the specimen readout with the ladder strip, once the tier list exists.FABLE
  7. Final native pass at 1920 × 1080: textures, backgrounds, ten-minute routes, no truncation, export.ASTRA
