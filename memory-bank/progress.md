# Progress

The concept and first interview answers are recorded. Two imagegen comparison sheets have been shown: initial world art directions, and cartoon/low-poly/voxel character reference views. These are generated references, not editable 3D models or in-game screenshots.

Blender is updated to 5.2.1 LTS for Apple Silicon, with CLI scene saving, GLB export, and rendering verified. Godot 4 standalone is selected, with personal play on the local Mac as the primary target. Untimed play, deferred damage, and roughly 3–5 minute levels are settled. The user proposed four representative levels across scales and vacuum-like liquid consumption; those details remain in the design interview. Godot installation, editable asset creation, gameplay implementation, and the single final visual/performance pass remain open.
