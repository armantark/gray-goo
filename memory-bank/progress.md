# Progress

The concept and interview answers are recorded. Imagegen sheets have shown initial world art directions, cartoon/low-poly/voxel character references, and a REST/ROLL/RELEASE study of a rounded sticky goo. These are generated references, not editable 3D models or in-game screenshots.

Blender is updated to 5.2.1 LTS for Apple Silicon, with CLI scene saving, GLB export, and rendering verified. Godot 4 standalone is selected, with personal play on the local Mac as the primary target. Untimed play, deferred damage, roughly 3–5 minute levels, local puddle consumption, a rolling sticky deformable goo, and a cosmic/spacetime campaign endpoint are settled. The precise four-level sampler and remaining motion/gameplay details are still in the design interview. Godot installation, editable asset creation, gameplay implementation, and the single final visual/performance pass remain open.
