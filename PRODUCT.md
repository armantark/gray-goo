# Gray goo game

## Register

product

## Platform

macos

## Users and purpose

The user plays locally on their Mac. The game offers untimed exploration through eating, growth, physical interactions, and playful changes of scale. The approved build has four representative scenes.

## Experience

Movement and eating must feel immediate. The goo is a sticky, deformable organism. Its body responds to ground contact and carries traces of the food it ate. The world stays readable as the goo grows.

## References

The original Tasty Planet informs eat-and-grow play, playful arrangements, and the nearest-edible pointer. Everything informs scale and variety of themes only. Smooth cartoon 3D, cel shading, and outlines are selected. Low-poly and voxel art are not selected.

## Interaction principles

Keep controls independent of body facing. Keep camera rotation optional. Let every bite contribute to the size goal. Preserve continuous local liquid surfaces. Celebrate the final bite without forcing the player to leave the scene.

## Readability

Use clear control labels and readable HUD text. Food color is visual feedback; the nearest-edible pointer and size goal must remain usable without color discrimination. The full accepted design is in memory-bank/systemPatterns.md.
